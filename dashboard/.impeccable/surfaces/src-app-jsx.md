## Direction contract
THESIS: Everything that matters on one screen as tight, ruled modules. Refuses the category's airy KPI-card grid and the old frosted "dusk glass" panels.
OWN-WORLD: White ground, black ink text, cool grey #E6E8EB module heads and fills, hairline #C9CDD3 rules, utility red #D7261E for module tabs, lost state and the active nav cap. Noto Sans JP at 12-13px body, 900 weight for figures. Square corners, no shadows, no blur.
STORY: Visitor opens on the Map view, sees the room with every tag, reads the asset table beside it, notices the red LOST row, clicks a tag to see its detail, and reaches calibration or receivers in one click.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Two-row top nav (brand, live state, receivers online, clock / section tabs with counts). Below, a mosaic: map module left full height; assets module (count strip + table) right top; receivers and activity modules side by side below. Primary action: selecting a tag (row or dot) fills a detail module.
